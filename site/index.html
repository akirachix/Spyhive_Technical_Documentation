    What is Bloom?

    Bloom is a maternal health platform designed to support mothers throughout pregnancy while connecting them with caregivers and Community Health Promoters.

    The platform brings together mobile technology, healthcare support, and administrative tools into one connected ecosystem.

    Bloom is designed to make maternal health information, symptom tracking, care scheduling, reminders, and communication more accessible and connected.

    Who is Bloom For?

    Mothers

    Bloom helps mothers manage their maternal health journey through symptom tracking, care schedules, reminders, pregnancy information, and maternal health resources.

    Caregivers

    Caregivers can stay connected with the mothers they support and access relevant maternal information shared with them.

    Community Health Promoters

    CHPs can support assigned mothers, monitor relevant information, receive alerts, and assist with maternal care.

    Administrators

    Administrators use the Bloom dashboard to manage users, maternal health content, platform metrics, safety alerts, and account settings.

    One Connected Ecosystem

    Bloom consists of several connected components that work together:

    • Bloom Mobile Application — the main application used by mothers, caregivers, and Community Health Promoters.

    • Bloom Backend API — provides application services, authentication, business logic, and data communication.

    • Bloom Administrative Dashboard — provides administrators with tools to manage and monitor the platform.

    • Bloom Informational Website — provides public information about Bloom and its maternal health services.

    These components communicate through defined APIs to provide a connected maternal health ecosystem.

    What Bloom Provides

    Maternal Health

    Access maternal health information and resources throughout the pregnancy journey.

    Symptom Tracking

    Record and monitor maternal symptoms and relevant health information.

    Care & Reminders

    Manage care schedules, appointments, medication reminders, and important dates.

    Connected Support

    Connect mothers with caregivers and Community Health Promoters when support is needed.
